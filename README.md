# Noxium

### Proof of Work. Rising Difficulty. Immutable Scarcity.

Noxium is a next-generation proof-of-work token built on **Robinhood Chain**.

Designed around computational scarcity, Noxium introduces a system where difficulty only moves upward, supply remains fixed, and every transaction contributes to a permanently evolving network.

No mint.
No reset.
No undo.

Only proof of work.

---

## Overview

Noxium combines the principles of proof-of-work mining with a taxed token economy on Robinhood Chain.

Unlike traditional tokens with adjustable supply mechanisms, Noxium is built around scarcity and irreversible progression.

The network becomes harder over time, rewarding early participation and preserving long-term scarcity.

---

## Core Principles

### ⛏ Proof of Work

Noxium is secured through computational effort.

Mining is not based on permission or allocation — it is earned through work.

---

### 📈 Difficulty Only Rises

The difficulty curve is designed to continuously increase.

Every stage of the network becomes more challenging, creating a growing barrier for future supply creation.

---

### 🔒 No Mint

Noxium has no hidden inflation mechanism.

No additional supply can be created after deployment.

---

### 🚫 No Undo

Transactions and network history are permanent.

What happens on-chain stays on-chain.

---

## Built on Robinhood Chain

Noxium is deployed on **Robinhood Chain**, leveraging its high-performance infrastructure and Web3 ecosystem.
